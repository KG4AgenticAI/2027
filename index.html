01

About the workshop

AI is moving beyond systems that generate content or answer single prompts towards agentic systems able to pursue goals, plan multi-step activities, interact with external tools and coordinate with other agents. As these agents become integrated with enterprise applications, business processes and organizational data, their development requires mechanisms to represent, access and integrate the knowledge their decisions and actions depend on.

KG4AgenticAI offers a dedicated venue to discuss the convergence of Knowledge Graphs and Agentic AI from an Information Systems Engineering perspective.

Scientific

Advance KG × Agentic AI research

Stimulate research on how Knowledge Graphs can provide semantic and contextual knowledge for engineering more advanced agentic AI systems.

Engineering

Methods and architectures

Explore methods and architectures for developing knowledge-driven intelligent Information Systems built on AI agents.

Community

Bridge two communities

Bring together researchers on KGs and LLMs, notably around the EU COST Action KGELL, with the systems engineering community of CAiSE.

02

Positioning

Within CAiSE

CAiSE covers knowledge-driven approaches, AI, KGs and agentic platforms broadly; KG4AgenticAI focuses on their convergence rather than treating them separately.

vs. hybrid AI venues

Workshops on hybrid AI address symbolic and sub-symbolic integration in general; KG4AgenticAI has a narrower focus on KGs as the knowledge backbone of agents.

vs. modeling venues

Conceptual models and business processes are welcome when they support the development or operation of agentic information systems.

All levels of research maturity are welcome: position and vision papers, experience reports and full research papers.

03

Topics of interest

  1. The convergence of Knowledge Graphs, Large Language Models and systems engineering
  2. Knowledge Graphs as mediators between data, stakeholders and large language models
  3. GraphRAG and Knowledge Graph-based retrieval for agentic applications
  4. Knowledge-driven tool selection and use, function calling and external system interaction
  5. Engineering and integration of knowledge-driven agentic Information Systems
  6. Multi-agent collaboration and coordination supported by shared Knowledge Graphs
  7. Context-aware agentic systems supported by Knowledge Graphs
  8. Temporal and contextual Knowledge Graphs for adaptive agentic systems
  9. Evaluation of Knowledge Graph-based Agentic AI systems
  10. Application scenarios for Knowledge Graphs and Agentic AI
  11. Empirical studies and experience reports on Knowledge Graph-based information systems
